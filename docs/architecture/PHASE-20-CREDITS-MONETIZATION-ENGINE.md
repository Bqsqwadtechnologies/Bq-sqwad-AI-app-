# Phase 20 — Credits & Monetization Engine
## Objective
Define one consistent credit ledger across AI features.
## Ledger
- Balance.
- Reservation.
- Consumption.
- Reversal.
- Reconciliation.
- Refund/adjustment events.
- Receipts.
- Usage history.
## Pricing
Generation/search/feature costs are catalog entries with effective versions. The APK never becomes the source of truth for prices.
## Acceptance
A failed generation cannot silently consume final credits; reservation and reversal are explicit server operations.
