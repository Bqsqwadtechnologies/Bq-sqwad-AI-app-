# Phase 4 — New Logo & Brand Asset Contract
## Objective
Reserve the brand layer for the new logo supplied by the owner. Do not import or reuse the old app logo.
## Asset contract
- Primary logo slot.
- Compact mark slot.
- Monochrome/light-background variant slot.
- Dark-background variant slot.
- Launcher/icon assets remain separate from in-app branding.
- Assets are referenced through a single brand resource contract so screens do not hard-code filenames.
## Rules
No generated replacement logo, old logo, website screenshot, or copied brand asset is accepted as the final brand identity.
## Acceptance
The app can receive the new logo later without changing feature screens, navigation, or backend contracts.
