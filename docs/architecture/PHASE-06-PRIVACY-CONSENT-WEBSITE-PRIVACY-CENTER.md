# Phase 6 — Privacy, Consent & Website Privacy Center
## Source of truth
Website privacy policy: https://bqsqwadai.com/privacy-policy
## Native privacy center
Provide dedicated sections for:
- Privacy policy.
- Data collection/use.
- AI processing.
- Camera permission.
- Microphone permission.
- Screen capture consent.
- Location permission.
- Notifications.
- Data export where supported.
- Account deletion.
- Third-party/provider processing.
## Consent
Sensitive capabilities require just-in-time Android permission and clear explanation. Consent state must not be treated as permanent authorization when Android requires a new consent session.
## Acceptance
Privacy links are configurable and versioned remotely, while the app remains usable when a policy document is temporarily unavailable.
