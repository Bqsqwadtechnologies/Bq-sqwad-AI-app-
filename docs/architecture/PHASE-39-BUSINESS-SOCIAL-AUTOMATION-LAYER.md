# Phase 39 — Business Social & Automation Layer
## Objective
Unify supported business channels behind capability-driven connections and workflows.
## Reference channel families
Social networks, messaging, email, CRM, booking, API, and communication channels supported by the product catalog.
## Automation
Workflow triggers, actions, approvals, scheduling, retries, and audit events are server-controlled.
## Agent interaction
Agents can use only the tools/channels granted to them by plan and permissions.
## Acceptance
Removing a channel permission immediately prevents the affected automation from executing.
