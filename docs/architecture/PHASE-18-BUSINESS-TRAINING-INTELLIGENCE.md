# Phase 18 — Business Training & Intelligence
## Objective
Give Business agents controlled knowledge and reference-data management.
## Supported reference classes
- Text.
- Documents.
- Images.
- Videos.
- Photos.
- Audio/voice.
- Product/settings documentation.
## Processing
Training/reference uploads are indexed server-side. The Android client manages uploads, status, metadata, and permissions.
## Rule
Training means controlled knowledge ingestion/configuration; it does not imply uncontrolled autonomous model retraining.
## Acceptance
Every knowledge item has ownership, status, source metadata, and removal behavior.
