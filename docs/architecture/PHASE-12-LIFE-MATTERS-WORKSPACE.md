# Phase 12 — Life Matters AI
## Objective
Create a dedicated Life Matters workspace with its own product identity, navigation, history, and pricing contract.
## Design
Use structured categories only where they improve navigation; avoid turning the screen into a wall of buttons.
## Safety
High-impact personal guidance must clearly distinguish AI assistance from professional services and provide appropriate escalation language when needed.
## Acceptance
Life Matters can be configured independently from Main Chat and Personal Intelligence.
